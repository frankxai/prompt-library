# Arcanea Worldbuilding Prompt Book Rubric

Score each prompt-book entry from 0 to 5.

## Domain Fit

- 5: Clearly Arcanea worldbuilding, lore, canon, media, APL, or studio workflow.
- 3: Creative but could be general or Starlight without stronger Arcanea anchors.
- 1: Mostly project operations, repo work, or generic swarm routing.

## Canon Safety

- 5: Separates confirmed, inferred, invented, and retcon material.
- 3: Mentions canon but does not ledger provenance clearly.
- 1: Treats unsourced invention as confirmed canon.

## Production Usefulness

- 5: Output is usable for writing, visuals, games, courses, public pages, or prompt books.
- 3: Interesting but needs another pass to become usable.
- 1: Vague atmosphere without constraints, details, or hooks.

## Retrieval Quality

- 5: Has clear title, tags, scope, purpose, inputs, output contract, eval notes, and related entries.
- 3: Discoverable by title but weak metadata.
- 1: Hard to search, route, or reuse.

Ship at 18/20 or above. Revise at 14-17. Rework below 14.
